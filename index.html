How Sunset Sideways works

The racing game for your browser

You drive a sports car along a three-lane boulevard. The car moves forward on its own and gets faster the further you go – you only steer left and right. Dodge taxis, convertibles, sedans, trucks, beacons and barriers and get as far as you can. Your best distance is saved in your browser.

The warm sunset turns into dusk and finally a neon night with a rising moon. Sunset Sideways is free, needs no download and no sign-up, and runs on computers and smartphones.

Controls

  • Computer: steer with the left and right arrow keys, start with Enter or Space, pause with Esc.
  • Smartphone and tablet: swipe your thumb left or right, or tap the left or right side of the screen (one lane per tap).

Power-ups

  • Pulse Shield: protects you from one crash.
  • Time Bubble: slows everything down.
  • Lane Flash: clears obstacles from your lane.
  • Magnet Field: pulls power-ups towards you.
  • Fly: lifts the car over the traffic.
  • Phantom Turbo: a short burst of speed.
  • Pathfinder: shows you the free lane.
